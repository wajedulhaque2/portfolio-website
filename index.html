Underwater Vehicle Hydrodynamics | RANS-VOF CFD in Star-CCM+

Structural Engineering Year 1

Electricity Pylon Design | Uganda Vaccine Plant

Designed and structurally analysed a 40 m S235 steel electricity pylon to power a TB vaccine production plant in Mbarara, Uganda. Tools: GSA (structural FEM), truss method of sections, hand calculations

Machine Learning Sports Analytics Personal Project · July 2026

2026 FIFA World Cup Prediction Framework

Built a full probabilistic forecasting framework for the 2026 FIFA World Cup quarter-finals from scratch. Engineered a leak-safe temporal feature pipeline using competition weighting, time decay, Elo ratings, and opponent-adjusted form across 49,505 historical international matches. Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost, and CatBoost under chronological validation - Logistic Regression won with a log loss of 0.8737, outperforming more complex tree ensembles. Assembled a weighted ensemble of the classifier, a Poisson GLM, and a Dixon-Coles score model, then ran 100,000 Monte Carlo tournament simulations. Diagnosed and resolved the "Argentina effect" where historical dominance, bracket path, and draw mechanics inflated one team's title probability despite weaker current squad metrics by redesigning the model around an explicit transparent team-strength table separating intrinsic strength, route difficulty, and knockout randomness.

Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, CatBoost, XGBoost, statsmodels, SciPy, NumPy, Matplotlib, Plotly

Final forecast: Spain 27.68% · France 20.96% · England 16.38% · Argentina 12.19%
